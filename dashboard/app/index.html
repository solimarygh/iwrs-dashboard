    About & methods

    Scope

    The corpus follows the scope of the Insect Welfare Research Society: insects, plus terrestrial invertebrates (arachnids, myriapods, gastropods) and aquatic invertebrates (crustaceans such as shrimp and prawns, horseshoe crabs, cephalopods). Horseshoe crabs are chelicerates, so they are grouped with arachnids, not crustaceans. Mites and ticks are not searched, because that literature is dominated by parasitology rather than the welfare of the mites themselves.

    What counts

    Peer-reviewed articles and reviews published 2016–2026 whose title or abstract names an invertebrate taxon and a state term (sentience, emotion or affective state, consciousness, welfare, pain, nociception, suffering). Retracted works are excluded.

    Nociception is shown separately

    Nociception, the detection of noxious stimuli, is not on its own evidence of sentience or a welfare focus. Papers whose only theme is pain or nociception, for example much of the Drosophila nociception neurogenetics literature, are hidden by default. Tick “Include nociception-only papers” to see them.

    Limits

    • Classification into taxa, themes and contexts is keyword-based and multi-label. Contexts are the least precise.
    • OpenAlex coverage of non-English and regional journals is better than most databases but still incomplete.
    • Precision and recall are estimated from a hand-coded random sample and a gold set of known papers; see Search & validation.
    • The current year is partial.

    Reproducibility & credit

    The corpus definition lives in config/query_config.yaml; an R pipeline retrieves and classifies the data, and this page aggregates it in your browser. The design of this dashboard is inspired by the Canadian Metaresearch Dashboard (COSSEE / SORTEE Canada), which in turn draws on the COKI Open Access Dashboard. Data: OpenAlex (CC0).
